Time Ledger

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Time Ledger (the “App”), developed by Douglas Barbosa (the “Developer”), handles information when you use the Android application.

1. Information you enter

Time Ledger allows you to record work sessions, including clock-in and clock-out times, breaks, and calculated working-time totals. These entries are stored locally on your device to provide the App's core features.

2. Collection and sharing

In the current offline version, the Developer does not operate an account system or a server that receives your work entries. The App does not intentionally transmit your recorded working-time information to the Developer or sell it to third parties.

This description covers the App’s own handling of entries. Device-level backup or diagnostic services provided by the operating system may operate separately under their providers’ terms and privacy policies.

3. Data storage and security

Work entries are stored on your device using local application storage. The App relies on the device's operating-system protections to help safeguard locally stored data. No method of storage is completely risk-free; keeping your device secure is important.

4. Data retention and deletion

Work entries remain in local storage until you remove them using available App deletion controls or delete the App's stored data. Uninstalling the App generally removes its local application data, although data preserved by device backups may be subject to your device provider's backup and restore settings.

5. Permissions and third-party services

The core offline time-tracking functionality does not require an account. If future versions introduce online services, analytics, advertising, or other integrations that change data handling, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those services before they are introduced.

6. Children's privacy

Time Ledger is a general productivity tool and is not specifically designed for children. The Developer does not intentionally collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service in the current offline version.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect product, operational, or legal changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
